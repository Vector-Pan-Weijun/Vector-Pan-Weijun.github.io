FEATURED RESEARCH

High-temperature oxidation of commercial alloys商业合金高温氧化行为研究

A comparative study of eight commercial alloys exposed to laboratory air at 1000°C for 400 hours. The workflow combined specimen preparation, mass-change tracking, optical cross-section imaging, surface observation and XRD phase matching. 比较八种商业合金在实验室空气中 1000°C、400 小时条件下的氧化行为，研究流程涵盖样品制备、增重跟踪、截面光镜观察、表面观察和 XRD 物相匹配。

03Observe观察Surface · cross-section表面 · 截面

Macroscopic surface comparison宏观表面对比

Selected specimens show visually distinct surface states after the same thermal exposure. The comparison establishes the macroscopic context before microstructural and phase-level analysis.相同氧化条件下，不同样品呈现明显不同的宏观表面状态，为后续截面组织和物相分析提供直观背景。

Cross-sections across magnifications不同倍数下的截面组织

20× images provide layer continuity and specimen context; 100× images reveal local scale morphology and subsurface features. Scale bars are retained from the original microscopy output.20× 图像用于观察氧化层连续性和整体形貌，100× 图像进一步呈现局部氧化层及近表面特征；所有图像均保留原始比例尺。
